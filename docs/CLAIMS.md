# Claim boundaries

## Allowed claims

- A preregistered threshold can remain binding even when the observed result is
  close to it.
- Contract, mechanism, behavior, throughput and experiment-readiness gates can
  disagree without contradiction.
- The published trial tables are sufficient to recompute the included decision
  gates.
- The ExecutionEvidence replay verifies deterministic offline isolation,
  ordering and idempotency on the included fixture.
- Under the frozen posture diagnostic, delay was the dominant observed
  bottleneck while the tested noise level made little descriptive difference.

## Disallowed claims

- The package proves a general improvement in robot control.
- The results are real-robot, neural or subjective-experience evidence.
- The GTSAM implementation failed behaviorally; its behavior gate was never
  evaluated in the interrupted batch.
- Passing the replay bus proves that feedback improves a policy or that a
  knowledge candidate is correct.
- The reconstructed ExecutionEvidence fixture is the original source dataset.
- A ready compute channel is evidence that the scientific prerequisites passed.

## Evidence levels

All preserved behavior results are historical, constructed-simulation evidence.
The readiness record is advisory. Reproduction of the decision scripts does not
upgrade either evidence level.
